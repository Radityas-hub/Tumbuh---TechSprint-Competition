## Style Prompt
Warm, modern, parent-centered healthcare product video for Tumbuh, a digital companion for parents of children with special needs. The mood is calm, hopeful, gentle, and grounded in daily family life. Use generous whitespace, soft green surfaces, rounded cards, subtle motion, and human photography. The video should feel reassuring rather than clinical or overly techy.

## Colors
- Deep green `#06443e` for primary text, buttons, and trust moments.
- Soft mint `#dff4ec` for supportive backgrounds and gentle emphasis.
- Warm off-white `#f8fbf9` for the main canvas.
- Muted text `#68766f` for secondary copy.
- White `#ffffff` for cards and clean overlays.

## Typography
- Use Poppins for UI and captions.
- Use high-weight Poppins for short emotional headlines, medium weight for body copy.

## Motion
- Smooth fade-up, soft scale reveals, slow photo parallax, and staggered card entrances.
- Keep animation calm and human; no glitch, flashy kinetic typography, harsh zooms, or aggressive transitions.

## What NOT to Do
- Do not imply Tumbuh diagnoses children or replaces doctors/therapists.
- Do not use dramatic medical emergency visuals.
- Do not use loud neon colors, heavy shadows, or busy dashboard clutter.
- Do not overanimate; motion should feel like breathing room.
